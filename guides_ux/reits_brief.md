## REITs
REITs are investment trusts that pool capital to purchase and own income-producing real estate. Properties are leased to tenants, and the resulting rental income is distributed to investors as passive dividends. REIT managers grow income through asset acquisitions, enhancement initiatives, or rental rate increases.
