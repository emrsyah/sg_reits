- **DPU linkage:** distribution policy (p163) distributes ≥90% of taxable income **"other than gains from the disposal of properties, and unrealised surplus on revaluation"** — i.e. **divestment gains are excluded from the distribution** (not a DPU booster here). No special/capital distribution of divestment gains disclosed. `distributed_gain = false`.
- **Use-of-proceeds** is disclosed only strategically (p32: "disciplined capital recycling strategy … maintain financial flexibility and liquidity … unlock gains from appreciation of asset values, and optimise returns for Unitholders") — not per-deal. Aggregate net proceeds S$434,984k (properties) recycled into the FY2025 acquisition programme; a separate S$500m private placement (p20/22) funded investments + debt repayment.
- **Recoverable but not captured (coverage gaps):**
