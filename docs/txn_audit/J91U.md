## Per-column null/value status

### sale_consideration — ✓ all correct (p47/p48)
Every headline sale price matches the "Sale Consideration (S$ million)" column exactly: 6.8 / 9.9 (completed p47); 101.0 / 42.2 / 34.1 / 16.7 / 3.5 / 15.1 / 45.0 / 68.0 / 113.5 (announced p48). 8-asset aggregate = S$338.1m (46A TP 113.5 + 24 JPR 68.0 + 21&23 Ubi 45.0 + 86&88 IR 42.2 + 120 Pioneer 34.1 + 13 Jalan Terusan 16.7 + 43 TVC 15.1 + 60 TSS1 3.5) ✓; grand total incl hotel + 2 completed = S$455.8m (p6/p39). ✓

### valuation — ✓ all correct, DISTINCT from carrying (no conflation)
Matches the p47/p48 "Valuation (S$ million)" column: 6.6 / 9.7 (completed, JLL as at **31 Dec 2024**); 100.9 / 42.2 / 34.1 / 16.7 / 3.5 / 15.1 / 42.5 / 68.0 / 109.5 (announced). **Valuation DATE nuance (correcting the assignment premise "each sold at its 30-Nov-2025 valuation"):** 8 of the 9 announced are valued **as at 30 Nov 2025** (Cushman/Edmund Tie/Colliers/JLL, footnotes 5–12, p48); the **hotel (2 Changi Business Park) is valued as at 31 Dec 2025 by Savills** (footnote 4, p48) — it is NOT a 30-Nov valuation. Figures themselves all correct.

### carrying_value — ✓ all genuine, RECONCILED (not a valuation-copy)
The per-asset carrying values are the year-end Portfolio-Statement "Independent valuation" (= balance-sheet fair-value carrying for a fair-value REIT). **Reconciliation proof:** the 8 announced carrying values sum to 41,409+33,440+16,383+3,410+14,814+41,700+66,792+111,498 = **329,446k ≈ Note 4 Trust "Investment properties held for divestment (excl ROU)" 329,447k** (p174); + hotel **97,300k** (explicitly printed carrying amount, p154) = **426,746k = Group held-for-divestment excl ROU** (p174). This proves carrying is the genuine printed year-end book value, sourced independently of the transaction valuation.
- **valuation ≠ carrying in all announced rows** (e.g. 86&88 IR: val 42.2 (30 Nov) vs carry 41.409 (31 Dec); 46A TP: carry 111.498 **exceeds** the 30-Nov val 109.5). Two different dates/sources → correctly kept separate. **No valuation↔carrying conflation.**
- Completed rows: 1 Third Lok Yang carry 6.6 = its 31 Dec 2024 valuation (same figure genuinely); 79 Tuas carry 8.722 (excl ROU, 31 Dec 2024) ≠ val 9.7 (incl ROU). Both are the last printed (31 Dec 2024) carrying before Mar-2025 divestment — genuine, but is prior-FY-end carrying, not an at-disposal book value.

### gain_on_divestment — ✗ ALL 11 DERIVED → propose null
No per-asset dollar gain is printed anywhere. The report prints only (a) per-asset **percentage** premiums for the two completed deals ("3.5% above valuation" / "1.5% above valuation", p16/p20) and (b) **aggregate** premiums ("2.3% above valuation" completed p16; "2.0% premium to independent valuation" for the 8-asset portfolio p47/p16). The Statement of Total Return shows a **net fair-value LOSS of S$125.1m** (p154) and no "gain on divestment" line at all. Every typed gain is sale−valuation (0 / 2.5m / 4.0m) or sale−carrying (hotel 3.7m) = derived → **null**.

## Suggestions / raw material for design report
- **DPU-boost signal — POSITIVE (ESR-LOGOS distributes divestment capital gains).** Distribution statement: **"Capital distribution" S$17,405k in FY2025** (up from S$11,571k FY2024) (p55). CEO review: FY2025 distributable income impacted by *"lower distribution of capital gains from the sale of investment properties in prior years"* (p56) — confirming capital gains from property sales ARE distributed to unitholders over time. Tax policy: capital gains *"may [be] distribute[d] … without having to deduct tax at source"* (p172). → `distributed_gain = true` (mechanism confirmed; FY2025 completed-deal gains likely feed future capital distributions). Strong DPU-linkage report.
- **Use-of-proceeds is disclosed** (unusually explicit): completed divestments' proceeds "redeploy … to pare down debt and fund AELs, redevelopments and future asset acquisitions" (p47); announced-portfolio proceeds assumed "used to repay debt" (p6 fn6, p39). Capture as `repay_debt` + `reinvest`.
- **Recoverable / coverage gaps:**
  - Per-asset $ gain NOT recoverable (only % premiums + aggregates) — do NOT derive.
  - **Valuation DATE + valuer name** disclosed per asset (p48 footnotes 4–12: 30 Nov 2025 for 8, 31 Dec 2025 Savills for hotel) — not captured; useful for a valuation-date field.
  - **Deal-cluster tag** missing: 8 rows belong to one announced portfolio (S$338.1m, 15 Dec 2025); hotel is a separate 30-Jan-2026 subsequent event; 2 completed are a third cluster. Table has no grouping/announcement-tranche field.
  - **carrying_value as-of-date** differs by row (31 Dec 2025 for announced; 31 Dec 2024 for the 2 completed) — a `carrying_as_of` field would disambiguate.
  - Expected-completion quarter (1Q/2Q/3Q 2026) disclosed per announced deal (p48) but not captured.
